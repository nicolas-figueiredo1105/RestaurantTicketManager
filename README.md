# Restaurant Ticket Manager
Restaurant POS systems can most of the times be complex and confusing. As a head-server, the amount of times that I had to stare at 
the screen to find the item I was looking for, could be used to give a better service to my customers. Or the times that I had to manually 
enter modifications, and again, waste time on something that could take a single touch. \
\
That is why I am developing Restaurant Ticket Manager. Inspired by my employer: 160 Main, I want to create a POS system that is 
intuitive, fast, and efficient.
